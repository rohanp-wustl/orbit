# Hackathon video script (~2 min)

Target structure from the brief: problem (~20 s) · demo story (~60–75 s) · how Ground Control
works (~15 s) · what's next (~10 s). Film on two real phones; screen-record + a hand-held shot of
the recipient's phone for the landing moment.

---

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem

**Visual:** A dorm room at night. A phone lights up with a group-chat photo, left on read. Cut to a
half-finished physical care package on a desk, tape and a shipping label, never sent.

**VO:** "When you move away, staying close means picking between two bad options. A text takes ten
seconds and feels like nothing. A care package means something, but it takes an afternoon and a
trip to the post office, so it never happens. We wanted the middle: something that feels
handmade, that you can make on a Tuesday night in five minutes."

## 0:20–1:30 — Demo story (Priya → Grandma Rose)

| Time | Visual | VO |
|---|---|---|
| 0:20 | Orbit sign-in: planet rising, "Orbit" with its orbiting moon | "This is Orbit." |
| 0:24 | Galaxy: Grandma's planet in close orbit, a ship crossing | "Your crew are planets. The more often you send each other things, the closer they orbit." |
| 0:30 | Create: gold package + rocket, Birthday, Sweet, 2 photos, a voice note, two lines of notes | "Priya's grandma turns 81. She adds two photos, a voice note, and a couple of lines: the cookies she can never get right." |
| 0:42 | Cosmo packing screen → canvas + drafts cards | "Ground Control, our AI, arranges it into a scrapbook page and drafts a few lines in her voice." |
| 0:50 | Tap **Redo** on a caption; edit another | "Every line is a card she can keep, edit, redo, or delete. It's her capsule, not the AI's." |
| 0:58 | Package loads into the gold rocket → 3-2-1 → liftoff → travel view | "Then she launches it." |
| 1:08 | Grandma's phone: lock-screen notification "Priya's capsule landed!" → rocket lands on her launchpad, drops the package | "It travels, and it lands." |
| 1:16 | Shake → burst → confetti → reveal card; voice note plays | "Unboxing is the point: the wait, the shake, the reveal, piece by piece." |
| 1:26 | Grandma's *Messages* app: Ground Control's text + the page + the voice note; she replies "The crust is all about cold butter!" with a photo → Priya's phone: a seafoam rocket lands, "via iMessage" | "Grandma doesn't even need the app. Ground Control brings the capsule to her texts, and her reply flies back as a rocket." |

## 1:30–1:45 — How Ground Control works

**Visual:** Split screen: the inputs (thumbnails, transcript, notes) → a JSON layout → the finished page.

**VO:** "Ground Control never generates images. It gets small thumbnails, an on-device transcript
of the voice note, and her notes, and returns a layout that places her real media and our
hand-drawn stickers. It only writes what her notes support, and it follows a banned-phrase list,
so no 'cherish' and no 'journey'."

## 1:45–1:55 — What's next

**VO:** "Next: home-screen widgets and push once we have a developer account, group capsules for
the whole family, and a web link so anyone can open a capsule without the app."

**End card:** Orbit logo · "Send a piece of your world." · team names · repo link.
